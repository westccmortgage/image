Using Wallet WCCM in ChatGPT or Claude

Wallet WCCM is available as an app inside ChatGPT and as a connector in Claude. Ask it things like “How much house can I afford in Los Angeles with $180,000 income and $120,000 saved?” or “What would my monthly payment be on a $1.1M home in Glendale with 10% down?”. The app calculates buying power, monthly payments, cash to close, loan program fit, wildfire rebuild financing options and document checklists for California.

Results are estimates for planning, not a rate quote, Loan Estimate, pre-approval or commitment to lend. Rates and program terms change daily. A licensed loan officer confirms real numbers after reviewing your file.

If you ask the assistant to connect you with a broker and agree to be contacted, your name, contact details and a short scenario summary are sent to us. To update or delete that information, email or call us using the details above. See our Privacy Policy.
